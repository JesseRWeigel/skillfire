What the counterfactual can and cannot see

Counting fires is easy. The number that would say a skill is dead weight is the one where its situation arose and something else was used, and a transcript has no field for what the model considered and passed over. The proxy used here is lexical: each skill's own frontmatter description, which is the only text about it in front of the model when it chooses, is reduced to distinctive terms, and a user turn carrying two or more of them counts as an opening.

The proxy is checked against the cases where the answer is known. Of the 10 sessions that really did fire a skill, 2 were flagged as an opening for the skill that fired, a recall of 20%. Read the opening counts below with that in mind. They are a weak signal, and the honest reading is that they bound the question rather than settle it.

A skill that fires rarely because its situation is rare is not dead weight. That is why the two never-fired groups below are separate tables and are never added together.
